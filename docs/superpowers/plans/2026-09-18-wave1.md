## Constraints

- Preserve all source activation, rights holds, evidence restrictions and production schedules.
- Unknown rights/date/cadence states remain explicit; a successful download is not fresh actionable evidence.
- Received, normalised, unique, held, acknowledged and uncertain counts have distinct meanings. Held is a subset, not an extra additive category.
- Unknown write results cannot be reported as zero or exact. Existing insertion intent remains authoritative.
- Receipts cover refresh pulls and backfill pages; reads and diagnostics do not ingest.
- No production deployment or new push is part of this batch. Verification aliases never ship to production.
