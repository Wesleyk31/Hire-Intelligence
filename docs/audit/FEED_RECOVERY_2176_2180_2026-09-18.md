## Changes

- `backend/feed-recovery.ts` routes the three title ZIPs through the existing bounded ZIP reader. Petroleum and geothermal use observed, explicitly listed schema/type combinations; unknown schemas and wrong-domain identifiers fail review. The mineral archive remains size-blocked and has no accepted row schema.
- IDs combine source, exact layer, `TITLEID` and `UNIQ_ID`. Application, granted, historical, pipeline and petroleum records therefore retain their separate domains, while distinct holder rows cannot overwrite each other. Conflicting duplicate identities still fail. `PTY_NAME` supplies the applicant/holder role, never a delivery-contractor claim.
- Eight geothermal system placeholders and ten reserve polygons are excluded from its title output. Petroleum excludes eleven placeholders and 1,016 explicitly identified reserve, release-area, regional or ALRA-context records. Exclusion is by verified schema or the exact documented provider sentinel, not by guessing missing IDs. A real title missing identity fails visibly.
- Each emitted row retains `CONTEXT_ONLY` and `SOURCE_RIGHTS_REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Provider `DT_EFFECT`, `DT_GRNT` and other fields remain raw. Collection time does not become a title event date. Existing live and historical paths share the parser and retain snapshot-checked continuation.
- `backend/source-helpers.ts` distinguishes the observed WAF challenge from an ordinary HTTP 202. Failed-response cancellation cannot stall on a cloned body reader. Stadiums checks only the same pinned workbook after an empty datastore; successful fallback pins that resource's workbook path for continuation. A conservative contract-reference/description field guard rejects metadata-only or incomplete rows. Even matching workbook rows retain `SOURCE_SCHEMA_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and `CONTEXT_ONLY` in live and historical normalization, including pinned workbook continuation. These synthetic-fixture checks do not establish acceptance of the actual challenged workbook's schema.
- Bounds remain 25 MiB compressed, 32 MiB expanded, 64 ZIP members, 20,000 placemarks and the existing 20-second source request deadline. The audit independently uses an 18-second deadline. No challenge workaround or broader retry/budget change was introduced.
