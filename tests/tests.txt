# Tests

## Test 1 - Public site is aggregate-only and operational workspace requires sign-in [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: public summary, Products, Solutions, Industries, Insights, About, secure workspace gate, sign-in/out transitions, duplicate authentication actions, GET /api/public/summary, protected GET /api/dashboard, five Pilbara feature photos, photo attribution
Description: Verifies public marketing pages remain usable without leaking project-level intelligence and the operational workspace is gated by authentication.

Steps:
1. Open the public homepage and navigate through Products, Solutions, Industries, Insights and About
   On the homepage, confirm all five feature cards display distinct real Pilbara photographs without broken images, stretching or overflow. Open Photo credits in the footer, confirm five source and licence entries, then use Back to Hire Intelligence to return.
2. Confirm public Insights shows aggregate project-signal, source and regional counts but no canonical project names, contractor names, CRM records or evidence provenance
3. Click Log in / Explore the platform and confirm a secure-workspace owner username/password form appears before Decision Desk. Empty or incorrect credentials must not open the workspace. If configured owner credentials are available through the secure test environment, sign in and confirm Decision Desk loads; never place real credentials in this test file or logs.
4. With a valid owner session, reload and confirm Remember me retains access after server verification. Sign out and confirm the workspace requires credentials again. Repeated clicks during a pending authentication action must not create duplicate requests; a failed sign-in must allow another attempt. A forged or revoked session must never load private data.

Expected: Public pages are aggregate-only; project-level workspace data is not returned until authenticated; successful sign-in opens the operational platform and sign-out restores the gate; authentication actions remain retryable without duplicate pending operations.
